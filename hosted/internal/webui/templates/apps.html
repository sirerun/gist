OpenAI + Airtable

A brief, a draft, a decision.

A content team could keep topics and source material together, prepare first drafts, then decide what deserves to move forward.

A workflow idea to configure in your client; this page does not activate a ready-made automation.

The shape of the work

Keep the human decision in the loop.

01

Read the brief

Select the Airtable records and fields the task may read: topic, audience, sources and status.

02

Prepare a draft

Use the chosen model to produce a draft grounded in those sources, with uncertainties called out.

03

Review before saving

Inspect the draft and proposed record changes. Approve the specific write only when you are satisfied.

A starting prompt for Zati

Help me plan a content review workflow using selected Airtable topics and OpenAI. First check which tools are connected. Read only the records I select, prepare a draft with source references, and show any proposed changes for my approval. Do not publish.

What you need

  • An Airtable connection with the required read tools; writing needs a separate allowed action.
  • An authorized model route and an agreed usage budget.
  • A Zati configuration with the required tool bindings, task scope and review steps.

Available operations depend on your accounts and connected tools. Gist does not supply provider access or execute this workflow.
